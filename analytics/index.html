Despite enforcement actions and product recalls, violation rates have trended sharply upward — from 12% in 2017 to 88% in 2023. 🚨 The situation is worsening, not improving. The share of samples exceeding the 1 ppm legal threshold rose from 12% in 2017 to 88% in 2023. Pakistan has displaced Mexico and India as the dominant manufacturing source, now attributed to 52–70% of contaminated products.
